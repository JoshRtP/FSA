# SAI Platform and FSA Document Instruction Guide

**Purpose:** Help farmers, Farm Management Group (FMG) Coordinators, buyers, sustainability managers, scheme owners, and verification-preparation teams understand which SAI Platform and Farm Sustainability Assessment (FSA) documents to use, when to use them, and how they fit together.

**Document set reviewed:** All FSA and SAI Platform source documents currently present in this repository, plus the repository’s direct-verification and benchmarking implementation plan, as of June 12, 2026. Always confirm that the version being used remains current before beginning a new assessment, benchmark, verification, or public claim.

**Word-compatible copy:** Open `SAI-Platform-FSA-Document-Instruction-Guide.rtf` in Microsoft Word or another compatible word processor. The RTF format is text-based so it can be reviewed and included in repository pull requests that do not support binary DOCX files.

## 1. Start Here: How the Documents Work Together

The FSA documents form a connected implementation and improvement system rather than a set of independent references.

1. Read the **FSA Implementation Framework** to define the purpose, scope, roles, management system, and verification approach.
2. Establish the farm or FMG, appoint responsibilities, and complete priority screening.
3. Complete the **FSA Self-Assessment Questionnaire (SAQ)** to assess farm practices and identify performance gaps.
4. Use the **FSA 3.0 Training Toolkit** to explain individual SAQ questions and expected evidence to farmers.
5. Use the **Continuous Improvement Module** to turn priorities and gaps into a documented Continuous Improvement Plan (CIP).
6. Use the **Outcome Measurement Handbook** to select tools and measure results such as greenhouse-gas emissions, soil health, biodiversity, and water outcomes.
7. Follow the **Implementation Framework** when preparing for verification and making verified FSA claims.
8. After an FMG receives a Letter of Attestation, use the **FMG Verification Audit Annual Reporting Template** at the required anniversaries.
9. If farms already participate in another sustainability scheme, check the **FSA Benchmarking Results** and use the **Benchmarking Protocol** before duplicating direct FSA implementation.
10. Use the **SAI Platform RTF–NIAB Database** as supporting evidence when selecting regenerative-agriculture practices, not as a replacement for FSA assessment or verification.
11. Use the repository’s **FSA Silver/Gold Benchmarking and Direct Verification Plan** when a team needs a decision-ready project plan, evidence checklist, governance structure, or route-to-claim workplan. It is an internal implementation aid, not an official SAI Platform document.

## 3. Recommended FSA Implementation Workflow

### Phase 1 — Define the program

- Agree why FSA is being implemented and what claims or customer requirements it must support.
- Define the crop, product, farm, FMG, geography, and volume scope.
- Appoint an FMG Coordinator or responsible person.
- Establish management, recordkeeping, sampling, and volume-accounting processes.
- Confirm whether direct FSA implementation is necessary or an existing benchmarked scheme can be used.

**Primary document:** FSA Implementation Framework V3.0.

### Phase 2 — Assess current practice

- Complete priority screening to identify locally material sustainability topics.
- Have the required farms complete the SAQ using Yes, No, or Not Applicable responses.
- Retain evidence for every Yes response and justification for every permitted Not Applicable response.
- Review the calculated performance level and topic-level gaps.

**Primary documents:** FSA Self-Assessment Questionnaire V3.0.1 and FSA 3.0 Training Toolkit.

### Phase 3 — Plan improvement

- Select meaningful priority topics using priority screening, SAQ gaps, and farmer/stakeholder input.
- Establish baselines and measurable targets.
- Define activities, owners, deadlines, monitoring, and adjustments.
- Record progress internally at least annually.

**Primary document:** FSA Continuous Improvement Module V3.0.

### Phase 4 — Measure outcomes

- Decide what outcome must be measured and why.
- Select an appropriate scale, methodology, and tool.
- Establish a consistent baseline and data-collection method.
- Repeat measurement consistently and use results to improve the CIP.

**Primary document:** FSA Outcome Measurement Handbook V3.1.

### Phase 5 — Verify and maintain claims

- Select an appropriate Verification Body and prepare the management system and sampled farms for audit.
- Retain the Letter of Attestation and supporting verification records.
- Submit FMG composition reports at the required year-one and year-two anniversaries.
- Monitor membership turnover and growth to determine whether another farm-performance audit is triggered.

**Primary documents:** FSA Implementation Framework V3.0 and FMG Verification Audit Annual Reporting Template.

### Route-planning aid — Silver/Gold benchmarking or direct verification

When the objective is specifically to achieve FSA Silver or Gold, use the repository implementation plan to decide between scheme benchmarking and direct farm/FMG verification, organize the evidence library, assign project responsibilities, and prepare a controlled submission or audit-readiness workplan. Confirm every requirement against the current official normative documents before execution.

**Supporting repository document:** `docs/FSA_SAI_SILVER_BENCHMARKING_PLAN.md`.

## 4. Document-by-Document Instructions

### 4.1 FSA Implementation Framework V3.0

**Repository file:** `fsa-implementation-framework-v3.0.-eng.pdf`

**What it contains**

- Purpose, scope, governance, and formal status of FSA.
- Roles of farms, FMGs, FMG Coordinators, buyers, Verification Bodies, and SAI Platform.
- Description of the SAQ, priority screening, continuous improvement, outcome measurement, verification, benchmarking, and supply-chain modules.
- Step-by-step implementation requirements.
- FMG setup, sampling, volume accounting, verification, performance claims, and Letters of Attestation.

**When to use it**

- Before starting an FSA program.
- When determining program scope and responsibilities.
- When setting up an FMG management system or volume-accounting system.
- When preparing for initial verification, re-verification, or claims.
- Whenever a question concerns a formal FSA implementation requirement.

**How to use it**

- Create a checklist based on the six implementation steps.
- Assign an owner and evidence location to each applicable requirement.
- Use it as the controlling implementation reference; supporting guides do not replace its requirements.

### 4.2 FSA Self-Assessment Questionnaire V3.0.1

**Repository file:** `fsa-self-assessment-questionnaire-version-3.0.1-eng-7.xlsx`

**What it contains**

- General farm-information questions.
- 109 numbered farm sustainability questions across 11 topics: Farm Management & Community; Plant Material Selection & Propagation; Soil Management; Nutrient Management; Chemical Control; Integrated Pest Management; Water Management; Biodiversity; Air Quality and Emissions; Waste Management; and Labour Conditions.
- Essential, Intermediate, and Advanced classifications.
- Requirements, smallholder requirements where applicable, guidance notes, links, glossary terms, and a Performance worksheet.

**Performance thresholds**

| Level | Essential | Intermediate | Advanced |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Bronze | 100% | At least 50% | No minimum |
| Silver | 100% | At least 75% | At least 50% |
| Gold | 100% | 100% | At least 75% |

**When to use it**

- To establish a baseline, identify gaps, and calculate indicative performance.
- Before developing the CIP and before verification.
- Periodically when practices or farm circumstances change.

**How to use it**

1. Complete the General Questions worksheet.
2. Answer all applicable questions in the SAQ worksheet.
3. Read requirements and guidance before answering Yes or Not Applicable.
4. Keep evidence for each Yes response.
5. Review the Performance worksheet and use identified gaps in improvement planning.

**Important:** Completing the SAQ alone does not authorize a verified Bronze, Silver, or Gold claim.

### 4.3 FSA 3.0 Training Toolkit

**Repository file:** `fsa-3.0-training-toolkit.pdf`

**What it contains**

- Visual, farmer-friendly explanations corresponding to the FSA questions.
- Examples of required practices, records, training, evidence, decisions, and controls.
- Practical guidance across land rights, farm management, inputs, soil, water, biodiversity, labour, safety, and other assessment topics.

**When to use it**

- During farmer onboarding and workshops.
- Before or while completing the SAQ.
- When a farmer needs help understanding a question or evidence expectation.
- When developing training or corrective/improvement activities.

**How to use it**

- Locate the slide matching the SAQ question.
- Review its examples with the farmer.
- Compare expectations with actual farm practices and records.
- Record gaps for action, but confirm formal requirements in the SAQ and Implementation Framework.

### 4.4 FSA Continuous Improvement Module V3.0

**Repository file:** `fsa-continuous-improvement-module-v3.0-eng.docx`

**What it contains**

- Guidance for developing and implementing a CIP.
- Farmer-engagement and outreach guidance.
- A CIP template and a progress-report template.
- A flexible five-step process: identify priorities; establish targets; develop activities; confirm timeline and accountability; monitor and adjust.

**When to use it**

- After priority screening and self-assessment.
- Before verification when documenting the required improvement approach.
- During annual progress review and re-verification preparation.

**How to use it**

- Engage farmers and stakeholders in selecting priorities.
- Define a baseline, target, due date, activities, and responsible people for each priority.
- Monitor progress, record lessons, and adjust the plan when needed.
- Maintain internal progress reports at least annually.

### 4.5 FSA Outcome Measurement Handbook V3.1

**Repository file:** `fsa-outcome-measurement-handbook-v-3.1_september2023.pdf`

**What it contains**

- Guidance on why, when, and how to measure outcomes.
- Criteria for choosing an appropriate measurement tool.
- Detailed sections on greenhouse gases and climate, soil, biodiversity, and water.
- Example calculator tools, including Cool Farm Tool, Fieldprint Platform, and Puma Platform.

**When to use it**

- When moving from practice assessment to measuring actual results.
- When selecting a calculator, metric, sampling approach, or baseline method.
- When evaluating whether CIP activities produce intended outcomes.

**How to use it**

1. Define the outcome, purpose, audience, and measurement scale.
2. Review the relevant outcome chapter.
3. Compare tools for scientific robustness, relevance, data needs, cost, transparency, and outputs.
4. Establish and consistently repeat the measurement method.
5. Feed results back into the CIP.

### 4.6 FSA Benchmarking Protocol V3.01

**Repository file:** `fsa_benchmarking-protocol-v-3.01.pdf`

**What it contains**

- The process for comparing certifications, company programs, legislation, national schemes, and diagnostic tools against FSA.
- Assessment of scheme content, governance, and assurance.
- Application, consultant review, results, claims, validity, fees, and governance requirements.

**Key distinction**

- **FSA Aligned:** Content has been benchmarked and governance requirements are met, but FSA minimum verification requirements are not met. FSA-equivalent volume claims are not allowed.
- **FSA Equivalent:** Content, governance, and assurance requirements are met. Equivalent-volume claims may be supported within the approved benchmark scope.

**When to use it**

- When an existing scheme may replace some or all direct FSA implementation.
- When a scheme owner seeks formal recognition.
- When interpreting aligned versus equivalent status or planning a benchmark.

**How to use it**

- Check existing Benchmarking Results first.
- Define the exact standard version, country, product, legislation combination, and intended claim.
- Follow the application process and use an approved benchmarking consultant.

### 4.7 FSA Benchmarking Results — December 12, 2025

**Repository file:** `fsa-benchmarking-results-12-12-25.xlsx`

**What it contains**

- FSA 3.0 and historical FSA 2.1 benchmark results.
- Expired results and equivalence-level explanations.
- Scheme name, version, benchmark date, scheme type, combinations, geography, product, claim type, question coverage, and performance level.

**When to use it**

- Before requiring farms to duplicate FSA assessment activities.
- Before commissioning a benchmark.
- When validating an FSA aligned/equivalent claim and its scope.

**How to use it**

- Match the exact scheme version, country/region, product, and required combinations.
- Confirm whether the result is Aligned or Equivalent and whether it is Bronze, Silver, Gold, Not Yet Bronze, or expired.
- Do not assume that a result for one version, product, geography, or combination applies to another.

### 4.8 FMG Verification Audit Annual Reporting Template

**Repository file:** `fsa-fmg-verification-audit-annual-reporting-template_1.0-1-1.docx`

**What it contains**

- The required report on FMG membership and size after a Letter of Attestation is issued.
- FMG and Letter of Attestation details, farm counts, current farm list, FMG declaration, and Verification Body review.

**When to use it**

- Within four weeks of the first and second anniversaries of the Letter of Attestation validity start date.

**Key thresholds**

- More than 10% turnover in FMG membership; or
- More than a 10% increase in the total number of farms.

If either threshold is exceeded, the Letter of Attestation is invalidated but retains validity for six months from the relevant anniversary to allow a new farm-performance audit. The management-system audit does not need to be repeated if completed within the previous three years.

**How to use it**

- Maintain a current farm register.
- Compare current composition with the audited group.
- Calculate turnover and growth, attach the current farm list, submit the report on time, and retain Verification Body confirmation.

### 4.9 SAI Platform RTF–NIAB Database 2025

**Repository file:** `sai-platforms-rtf-niab-database-2025.xlsx`

**What it contains**

- A methodology, collated evidence table, glossary, and practice-specific evidence worksheets.
- Evidence concerning practices such as reduced soil disturbance, controlled traffic, cover crops, mulching, intercropping, crop rotation, habitat protection, agroforestry, buffers, grazing, manure, nutrients, pest management, irrigation, feed, and herd/flock management.

**When to use it**

- When selecting evidence-supported regenerative-agriculture activities for a CIP.
- When reviewing likely links between practices and outcomes.

**How to use it**

- Read the Introduction, Methodology, and CollatedTable worksheets first.
- Review the worksheet for the practice being considered.
- Use the evidence to inform decisions, not as a substitute for FSA assessment, verification, or outcome measurement.

### 4.10 FSA Silver/Gold Benchmarking and Direct Verification Plan

**Repository file:** `docs/FSA_SAI_SILVER_BENCHMARKING_PLAN.md`

**Status**

- This is a repository-created implementation plan based on official SAI Platform documents.
- It is not an official SAI Platform document, approval, legal opinion, or substitute for current normative requirements.

**What it contains**

- A decision framework for choosing between scheme benchmarking and direct FSA verification.
- A plan for pursuing Benchmark Equivalent Silver/Gold or a verified farm/FMG Silver/Gold result.
- Document hierarchy, scope controls, evidence-library requirements, crosswalk quality rules, gap analysis, governance, submission, verification-readiness, claims, and maintenance activities.
- Explicit distinctions among FSA Aligned, FSA Equivalent, direct verification, and a Letter of Attestation.
- References to additional official materials that may need to be obtained from SAI Platform when they are not present in the repository.

**When to use it**

- At project initiation when the target is Silver or Gold.
- When deciding whether an existing scheme should be benchmarked or farms should use direct FSA verification.
- When building a workplan, responsibility matrix, evidence library, benchmark crosswalk, or audit-readiness plan.
- When preparing claim language and controls after a successful result.

**How to use it**

- Begin with the route decision: benchmarking or direct verification.
- Follow only the relevant route while using the official documents as the controlling requirements.
- Convert its phases, evidence lists, and control points into a project tracker.
- Before relying on a requirement or claim statement, confirm it against the current official version supplied or confirmed by SAI Platform.

## 5. Official Documents Referenced but Not Currently Stored in This Repository

The repository implementation plan identifies several additional official FSA materials that can control or materially support implementation but are not currently present as separate files in this repository. Obtain and confirm current versions with SAI Platform when relevant:

- **FSA User Guide:** broad user-facing explanation of FSA routes and operation.
- **Guide to Making FSA Statements and Claims:** controls permitted claim language and claim checks.
- **Verification Body and Auditor Requirements:** controls qualification and approval requirements for verification providers and auditors.
- **Applicable FSA Audit Guides and Audit Control Points:** provide detailed verification and audit criteria.
- **FSA Topic Guides:** provide deeper topic-level guidance supporting SAQ interpretation and improvement.
- **Priority Screening Module/tool guidance:** supports structured identification of locally important priorities.
- **Supply Chain Implementation Guidance:** supports supply-chain use, volume accounting, and claims where applicable.

Do not assume a repository-created summary or plan replaces these materials. Record the version received and add it to the controlled project document register.

## 6. Responsibilities by Role

### FMG Coordinator

- Use the Implementation Framework to establish and operate the management system.
- Coordinate priority screening, SAQ sampling/completion, training, CIP development, verification, volume records, and annual reporting.
- Maintain evidence, farm registers, progress reports, and communication with the Verification Body.

### Farmer or Farm Manager

- Complete the SAQ accurately and retain supporting records.
- Participate in training, improvement planning, and outcome-data collection where applicable.
- Inform the FMG Coordinator when practices or farm circumstances change.

### Buyer or Sustainability Manager

- Define the business purpose and required claims.
- Check benchmarked schemes before requiring direct FSA implementation.
- Support resources for improvement and outcome measurement.
- Ensure external claims match verified or benchmarked scope.

### Scheme Owner

- Use the Benchmarking Protocol and Results to understand recognition options.
- Maintain scheme governance, assurance, and current documentation.
- Seek review when meaningful scheme changes could affect the benchmark result.

### Verification-Preparation Team

- Use the Implementation Framework as the primary requirements reference.
- Verify that SAQ answers, management-system records, CIP evidence, farm lists, volume records, and claims are complete and consistent.

## 7. Records to Maintain

A well-managed FSA program should maintain, as applicable:

- Defined program scope and objectives.
- FMG membership and farm register.
- Roles, responsibilities, and coordinator appointment.
- Priority screening outputs.
- Completed SAQs and supporting evidence.
- Training records.
- CIP and annual progress reports.
- Outcome-measurement method, baseline, input data, and results.
- Sampling records.
- Volume-accounting records.
- Verification reports and Letter of Attestation.
- Year-one and year-two annual FMG reports and Verification Body confirmations.
- Evidence supporting any benchmarked-scheme claim.
- Controlled document register showing the official versions used and any additional SAI Platform materials obtained for the project.

## 8. Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Treating the Training Toolkit as a replacement for formal SAQ or Implementation Framework requirements.
- Making a verified performance claim based only on a completed SAQ.
- Using Not Applicable without checking whether it is permitted and keeping a justification.
- Assuming a benchmark applies to a different standard version, geography, product, or legislation combination.
- Confusing FSA Aligned with FSA Equivalent.
- Using an equivalent claim outside the approved benchmark scope.
- Creating a CIP without measurable targets, owners, deadlines, monitoring, or farmer engagement.
- Changing outcome-measurement methods between baseline and follow-up without documenting the impact.
- Missing the year-one or year-two FMG annual reporting deadlines.
- Failing to monitor whether FMG turnover or growth exceeds 10%.
- Treating the repository Silver/Gold implementation plan as an official or normative SAI Platform document.

The documents in this repository include different versions and dates. In particular, the Benchmarking Results workbook is dated December 12, 2025. Before beginning an assessment, verification, benchmark, or public claim:

- Retain the version used as part of the program records.
